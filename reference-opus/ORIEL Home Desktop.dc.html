I · The Maison

We do not chase the season. We build the wardrobe that outlives it.

Founded in 1994 in the ninth arrondissement, ORIEL works in short series from a single atelier. Every garment is cut by hand, fitted on the body, and finished by the same tailor who began it. Nothing leaves the house untried.
